More ship controls, a self destruct alarm, and layouts for phones and other tablets.

- Flight: Eject, Self destruct and Emergency exit as red hold-to-confirm keys along the bottom; pitch/yaw lock, port lock, cargo loading request, and small cabin buttons for doors, locks and wipe visor.
- Self destruct pulses the whole screen red on every tab with a Hold to cancel button. It clears when you cancel, leave the seat, die, or after 3 minutes.
- Combat: weapon presets, gimbal lock, precision targeting, staggered fire, aim mode, sub-targets, auto targeting, look ahead, pin slots, decoy panic and arm fewer missiles.
- Phones: short tab names in portrait and a tighter layout held sideways; panels scroll when they don't fit.
- New sounds for the alarm, the cancel and eject.
