First installable version.

- Runs in the system tray instead of a console window, and updates itself from GitHub.
- Your keybinds and ship data now live in %APPDATA%\SC Dashboard, so updates never overwrite them.
- The iPad page reloads by itself after the PC app updates, and shows the version next to SC // DASH.
- The power screen clears when you die or your ship is destroyed, and a respawned ship starts from its default pips.
